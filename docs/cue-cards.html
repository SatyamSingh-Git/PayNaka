the hook

Razorpay gave AI agents thirty-five tools.

Then switched off the three that move money out.

local-only on the hosted server
create_refund disabled remotely create_instant_settlement disabled remotely close_qr_code disabled remotely

Two of those three are the ones that take money out of a merchant's account.

the thesis

Not an oversight. A trust boundary drawn by hand — by engineers who understood the risk and had no mechanism to manage it.

We built the mechanism that turns them back on.

what we refuse to claim

Prompt injection is not solved here.

Because that was never the goal.

Prompt hardening is a probabilistic defence against an adversary with unlimited attempts — it helps, and then it fails. We do not make the model impossible to persuade. We make persuasion insufficient.

Compromise the agent. Inject the prompt. Convince the model. It still cannot move money outside the signed mandate.

Anyone who tells you otherwise is selling something else.
the attack a better model cannot avoid

The merchant changes the price after the agent reads it.

reprice
the agent is not told

No injected text. No reasoning error. Nothing for a careful prompt to be suspicious of — the page simply says something different by the time the money moves.

the line

Diligence after an irreversible action is a post-mortem.

The only check that helps is the one that happens before the money moves. PayNaka's mandate is signed before the agent ever sees the page — so the reprice never has to be noticed to be refused. Nine times out of nine, at envelope.total_exceeded.
